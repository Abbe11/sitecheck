Make one that lasts centuries

  • Length beats complexity. A 5-word passphrase like correct-battery-horse-staple is far stronger, and easier to remember, than P@ss1!. Every extra character multiplies the guessing work.
  • One password per site. Reuse is how one breach becomes ten (credential stuffing).
  • Use a password manager. Let it generate and remember long random passwords; you memorize one master passphrase.
  • Turn on 2FA. Even a stolen password fails without the second factor.

Why cracks in seconds?

Attackers do not type guesses, they run offline lists against leaked password hashes at billions of tries per second (the hashing idea from the crypto block). Common passwords are pre-computed, so they fall instantly. A salt and a slow hash like bcrypt make this expensive, but that is the site job; yours is length and uniqueness.

Runs 100% in your browser. The strength meter sends nothing. The breach check sends only a 5-character hash prefix (k-anonymity), never your password.
